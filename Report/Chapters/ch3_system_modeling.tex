\section{Modified IEEE 13-Node Feeder Architecture}
This thesis builds on the modified 4.16~kV IEEE 13-node distribution feeder established in~\cite{Schulte2026,Mughni2026}. The feeder connects to an equivalent transmission grid at the Point of Common Coupling (Bus~632) and integrates four inverter-based solar PV systems via 4.16~kV/480~V step-up transformers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Active Inverter Buses ($\mathcal{G}$, 4 nodes):} \texttt{Bus 634}, \texttt{Bus 672}, \texttt{Bus 675}, and \texttt{Bus 680}.
    \item \textbf{Passive Load Buses ($\mathcal{P} \setminus \mathcal{G}$, 8 nodes):} \texttt{Bus 611}, \texttt{Bus 632}, \texttt{Bus 645}, \texttt{Bus 646}, \texttt{Bus 652}, \texttt{Bus 671}, \texttt{Bus 684}, and \texttt{Bus 692}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Supervisory Inverter Abstraction and Physical Constraints}
Consistent with~\cite{Schulte2026,Mughni2026}, inner current and voltage loops of the voltage source converters (VSCs) are assumed internally stable and well-damped. The reinforcement learning controller operates strictly at the supervisory layer, issuing reactive power setpoints $Q_g(t)$ (and active power curtailment $P_g(t) \le P_{g,\text{avail}}(t)$ only as a last resort).

Each inverter $g \in \mathcal{G}$ must obey its circular apparent power capability at every control step:
\begin{equation}
    |Q_g(t)| \le \sqrt{S_{g,\text{rated}}^2 - P_g(t)^2}
    \label{eq:apparent_power_limit}
\end{equation}
All monitored active and passive buses $p \in \mathcal{P}$ are required to remain within statutory voltage limits:
\begin{equation}
    0.95\text{ p.u.} \le V_p(t) \le 1.05\text{ p.u.}, \quad \forall p \in \mathcal{P}
    \label{eq:voltage_limits}
\end{equation}
